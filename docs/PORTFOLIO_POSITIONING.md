# Portfolio Positioning

## Technical identity

I build practical software across AI, healthcare technology, Web3, frontend engineering, mobile integration, data infrastructure, and automation.

## What I want a reviewer to see

- I can turn product ideas into working interfaces and systems.
- I can connect web applications to mobile runtimes through Android WebView bridges.
- I can work with Kotlin and Java as well as browser technologies.
- I understand relational data design and MySQL-backed application infrastructure.
- I treat telemetry and observability as part of the system rather than an afterthought.
- I use AI as an engineering layer: agents, tool calling, structured decisions, research assistance, and workflow automation.
- I understand the difference between a prototype, a research system, and a production claim.

## AI direction

The AI portfolio is intentionally hands-on. Example systems include tool-calling agents, AI-assisted market-data analysis, paper-trading workflows, and research pipelines.

For financial automation, examples default to paper/sandbox execution and include risk controls. For biomedical research, examples focus on literature and evidence workflows rather than claiming medical discoveries.

## Product direction

PharmWeb3 connects pharmacy knowledge with software. The broader product direction explores AI-assisted triage, pharmacist handoff, health education, financial assistance, and carefully scoped Web3 mechanisms.
