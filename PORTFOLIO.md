# Engineering Portfolio

This repository is the technical index for my broader portfolio.

## 01 — PharmWeb3

**Focus:** healthcare technology, AI, Web3

PharmWeb3 explores free and accessible healthcare guidance, AI-assisted triage, pharmacist handoff, health education and financial support workflows.

**Public product:** https://pharmweb3.com/

**Engineering themes**
- AI-assisted workflows
- pharmacist handoff
- token / reward infrastructure
- privacy-aware architecture
- product and partner APIs
- telemetry
