# AI Trading Labs

Research-oriented trading systems demonstrating how AI can sit on top of market-data and execution infrastructure.

## Crypto arbitrage

The arbitrage workflow compares prices across venues, accounts for fees and estimated slippage, scores an opportunity, and routes the result into a paper-execution layer.

## Forex agent

The forex workflow processes structured market inputs, asks an AI agent to classify the setup and explain its reasoning in structured form, then applies deterministic risk checks before creating a paper order.

## Important boundary

These are engineering and research systems. Live execution is not enabled by default and no profitability is implied.
