# AI-Assisted Cancer Research Workflow

A future-facing research engineering track for applying AI to biomedical literature and data workflows.

## Intended use

- literature discovery
- evidence extraction
- study comparison
- structured knowledge bases
- reproducible analysis notebooks/pipelines
- hypothesis generation for human researchers

## Scientific boundary

This project is a research-tooling concept. It does not claim to discover, validate, or provide a cure for cancer. Any medical conclusion would require appropriate datasets, scientific validation, clinical research, and expert review.
